PhD Candidate, ECE at UIUC

Hi, I'm Argy.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning, post-training methods for LLMs and multimodal models, and reasoning in language and multimodal systems.

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Venugopal V. Veeravalli. Previously, I earned a Diploma (BSc+MSc) in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Patras, where I worked with Prof. George Moustakides. As an intern, I worked as an AI Research Intern at Netradyne, developing reinforcement learning methods for autonomous driving, and as a Machine Learning Engineer Intern at Uber, working on transfer learning and reinforcement learning for fraud decision-making.
